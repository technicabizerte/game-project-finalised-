// The story

The Tailies is a Snowpiercer-inspired side-scrolling action game. You begin in the frozen squalor of the train’s tail section and fight forward, wagon by wagon, toward the engine — and the unjust order that keeps the last car starving in the cold.

Five hand-built wagons stand between you and the front, each a different world of privilege and each one defended. Master frame-by-frame melee and ranged combat, choose your fighter and weapon, and break the chain of cars one brutal battle at a time.

It isn’t just a fight. It’s an argument about who the train was built for.
